## Sources

- [Zoho Procurement](https://www.zoho.com/us/procurement/help/purchase-orders/approvals-for-purchase-orders/): Configurable simple, multilevel and custom approval flows with rejection and resubmission.
- [SAP Ariba Buying and Invoicing](https://www.sap.com/products/spend-management/procure-to-pay.html): Guided buying, supplier catalogs, policy controls and procurement/invoice integration.
- [HubSpot Sales Hub](https://knowledge.hubspot.com/forecast/use-the-forecast-tool): Forecast workbench grounded in pipeline and forecast categories.
- [Microsoft Dynamics 365 Sales](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/sales/configure-forecast): Forecast hierarchy, access permissions, quotas, configuration and drilldowns.
- [HubSpot CRM](https://www.hubspot.com/products/custom-objects?aid=1280): Custom objects extend the standard customer data model.
- [HubSpot Marketing Hub](https://www.hubspot.com/products/marketing/advanced-marketing-reporting): Marketing reporting connects customer journeys and attributable revenue.
- [Xero](https://www.xero.com/id/accounting-software/reconcile-bank-transactions/): Connected-bank reconciliation with rules, matching and review of uncertain matches.
- [Moneybird](https://www.moneybird.com/features/bookkeeping/): Invoice categories, financial reports, imports and supported tax-return submission.
- [Power BI](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/power-bi/connect-data/service-real-time-streaming): Documented streaming visualization architectures; roadmap and availability must be checked for a selected deployment.

- [Front](https://help.front.com/en/articles/2216): Shared drafts follow inbox access and support collaborative editing.
- [Zapier](https://zapier.com/blog/human-in-the-loop-guide/): Human review can pause automated flows before continuation.
- [n8n](https://docs.n8n.io/integrations/builtin/core-nodes/n8n-nodes-base.errortrigger): Official Error Trigger documentation is indexed. Full content retrieval was unavailable; no detailed parity claim is based on it.

## Research limits

Coupa documentation returned an unsupported-browser page. n8n full-page content retrieval failed; its indexed official documentation only establishes the limited Error Trigger reference. Neither is treated as complete benchmark evidence. Browser tests on local Foundly were blocked by the cloud browser URL policy. No paid competitor access, trials, user credentials or copied product code was used.
