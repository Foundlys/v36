# Native Finance configuration in the priority demos

Both newly generated Automotive and E-commerce universes now create their fictional Finance business through the existing native service: one EUR legal entity, one fiscal period for each calendar year touched by the scenario, and the nine native ledger account roles. Names explicitly identify the synthetic scenario; VAT and registration identifiers are `DEMO-NOT-REGISTERED`. A configured ledger does not prove revenue, paid invoices, a bank connection, actual registration or a complete financial history.

The default 18-month September 2026 scenario adds twelve nodes. Automotive now has 5,593 nodes and E-commerce 3,587. The bounded E-commerce HTTP case has 251. Existing confirmed manifests keep their original node counts, fingerprints and applied records; generator changes never replace those graphs. Earlier profiles and full-load measurements remain evidence of their recorded source and graph only.

The new Law contract permits only native legal-entity, fiscal-period and ledger-account creation. The engine calls the normal guarded Finance methods with stable native idempotency keys. Normal module/capability and native role checks apply before every operation and source read. There is no direct insertion of rows, receipt fabrication, provider connection or permission promotion.

Fiscal periods and accounts have no native revision field. Their engine references record a null revision and a hash of the entire current native row. No revision is invented; changed dependencies still fail before another effect. Legal entities retain their actual revision. Finance lookup uses the native authorized paginated list and bounded, monotonic cursors.

The real encrypted E-commerce HTTP case now uses the seeded Finance configuration for its ZERO invoice/post/payment chain. Their economic dates remain separate from actual native audit time. No extra Finance test entity or manual chart setup is substituted. No external payment or message is sent.
