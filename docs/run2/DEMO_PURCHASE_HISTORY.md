# Native purchase approval and demo operating expenses

Both priority demo generators now add eighteen separate fictional operating-service invoices over their default eighteen-month horizon. Four remain pending approval, four approved but unposted, four posted and unpaid, three partially paid and three paid. Native Finance records ten expense postings and six separate outgoing internal payment bookings. Each invoice references an actual native fictional supplier and the native demo legal entity. The recorded actor and audit time remain the authenticated execution identity and actual time; scenario dates and persona attribution do not impersonate a second approving person.

The examples book operating expenses, input tax and payables. They do not commit existing procurement proposals, invoice opening stock, capitalize vehicle acquisitions, establish inventory cost or produce verified bank evidence. Automotive and E-commerce default manifests now contain 5,983 and 4,073 nodes. Older retained and confirmed graphs preserve their original fingerprint, nodes and request identities. The bounded twelve-order E-commerce HTTP graph contains 318 nodes, 26 invoices and nine payments before the separate additional ZERO sales invoice. After that extra invoice/post/payment it has 27 invoices, ten payments and 28 balanced journals.

`INVOICE_APPROVE` is a distinct native, HTTP and structured ZERO action. Preparation binds the exact current purchase invoice, legal entity and all invoice lines, validates line calculations and totals, and requires explicit reason and confirmation. The review displays supplier, document number and every line's description, quantity, unit price, net amount, tax rate and gross amount. Eight new keys cover all eight existing locales and preserve source text as literal text. Approvers can prepare, retain, execute, recover and acknowledge approvals without gaining creation, posting or payment authority. Accountants can create and post but cannot approve. Dispatch routes admit reading the command; current typed module, capability and core approval authority are rechecked after body parsing before any effect.

Native approval seals the current source. Purchase posting checks that seal again, including direct native posting. An unversioned line, supplier or legal-entity change invalidates approval for future posting. Legacy approved drafts are preserved but need explicit renewed approval before a new posting; there is no automatic approval or metadata migration. Already posted records and prior receipts are retained. Creation cannot inject the approval seal. Approval itself creates no journal or payment.

The existing encrypted confirmation retention and explicit recovery flow also handles approval-only owners. A physically lost ZERO response remains recoverable after restart and later posting. Original approved draft and current posted invoice are shown separately. An owner whose capability is revoked during the real HTTP body await cannot complete the approval; the pending invoice remains unchanged. Per-action native idempotency and retained demo preparations prevent duplicate approval, posting or payment after a lost demo cursor.
