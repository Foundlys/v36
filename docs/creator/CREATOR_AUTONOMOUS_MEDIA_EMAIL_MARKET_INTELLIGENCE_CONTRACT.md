Every creator has a private, personal ZERO dashboard on supported desktop and iOS/mobile. ZERO greets with source-backed TODAY/WEEK opportunities, inbox triage, real commercial obligations, creator calendar, content milestones, edits ready for review and expected achievable tasks. Same ZERO conversational, socially intelligent, empathic, natural-voice and authorization behavior previously required globally, no imitation of actual consciousness.

Example, only when connected real data actually supports it:
- "Your recent short-form videos on topic X had stronger measured average engaged view rate than comparable uploads. Licensed source Y shows rising interest in your target country. Film script A today, variant B tomorrow; two existing clips can be repurposed." 
- ZERO inspects the creator's *own previously published and consented uploaded media*, drafts a platform-specific editable vertical cut, proposes distinct on-brand hook/caption/thumbnail options, renders a draft, and schedules an authorized publishing slot.
- ZERO classifies a genuine sponsor inquiry, checks actual agreed rate card, exclusivity, content obligations and schedule, replies automatically only if **safely pre-authorized**, and records a verified email-send receipt. Commercial/contractual commitments must be approved.

No promises of specific views, virality, followers or revenue. No "opportunity" or "last week" claim without time-stamped, accessible source data and calibrated uncertainty.

## C. REAL MARKET-DATA OPPORTUNITY ENGINE — daily and weekly

**Inputs (only as licensed/authorized/available):**
1. Creator first-party account analytics, historic publish/performance and channel/audience data from provider-approved APIs; never substitute simulated provider results.
2. Current niche/location/language/topic/search momentum from accessible licensed trends/search providers. Google Trends API is alpha/invite-only until confirmed otherwise, so **cannot be assumed available**.
3. TikTok Creative Center/Trends and other publicly viewable trend signals only via compliant permitted use; website visibility DOES NOT grant authorized high-volume API scraping. TikTok Research API is researcher-restricted, **not** a default commercial ingestion API.
4. YouTube channel/video analytics via creator OAuth where applicable (views, engagement, retention/watch duration, traffic source, follower changes and other actually accessible metrics), plus compliant approved metadata from other channels.
5. Brand deals, posting contractual obligations, upcoming events/holidays relevant to region, creator calendar/availability, available/raw media footage and editorial safety.

**Workflow:**
- Refresh sources as frequently as license, source rate limit, latency budget and actual data cadence allow; record observation and ingestion timestamps, permissions and missing fields; avoid labels like "live" for delayed metrics.
- Compare trend movement and creator historical performance with cohort/format baseline and statistical uncertainty. Normalize platform metric definitions and observation windows, avoid cross-platform unqualified views/engagement comparisons and future-information leakage.
- Score each possible concept with transparent, editable rubric: creator/content-fit, current evidenced demand/momentum, historical viewer response, saturation/fatigue, originality, production effort, risk/legal/brand compatibility, deadlines and confidence/data quality.
- For each proposed opportunity, produce a **source-linked evidence card**: exact observed metric/window, assumptions, relative rationale, uncertainty, confidence classification, expected effort, recommended format/length/hook/shot list/caption/thumbnail/voice language, assets already on hand, recommended publication window and permission requirements.
- Every morning: a ranked actionable **TODAY** brief (3–5 materially differentiated video/photo/story ideas). Every week: a calendar-aware **WEEKLY** plan with realistic production tasks, 7-day schedule, repurpose queue, sponsor deadlines and capacity balance.
- Do not endlessly chase trend content: balance brand identity, sustainable cadence, audience fatigue, niche consistency, original content and sponsor obligations.
- Outcome loop: capture only authorized actual post performance, compare to comparable historic posts, record prediction error/confounding, recalibrate opportunity model and editing strategies. A forecast score is **not** an empirically calibrated "virality probability" until validated prospectively.
- A/B variant experiment plans may be prepared, but publication and outcome tracking respect provider limitations; do not label observational comparison as causal proof.

## D. Fully automatic VIDEO EDITOR — personalized to genuine opportunity

**Must use both** real opportunity signals from C and the creator's actual media/style/history from B. Not just "auto trim and add captions".

**End-to-end path:**
`AUTHORIZED IMPORT -> MEDIA ANALYSIS -> CREATOR CONTENT DNA -> MARKET OPPORTUNITY -> SHOT/HOOK CANDIDATES -> RANK -> EDIT DECISIONS -> REAL NON-DESTRUCTIVE TIMELINE -> RENDER VARIANTS -> TECHNICAL/CONTENT QC -> PREVIEW/APPROVE OR ALLOWED AUTO-PUBLISH -> VERIFIED PLATFORM RECEIPT -> OUTCOME LEARNING`.

**Required native editor capabilities, benchmarked per relevant feature:**
- Multi-format ingests: shorts/long form, multicam, vertical/horizontal, variable frame rate, speech, different sample rates and high-resolution footage. Video timeline must be truly editable, with safe version history, undo/redo and non-destructive assets; never just show a simulated export.
- Scene and speaker detection, dialogue transcription/timecodes, speech/beat/hook salience, purposeful remove silence/fillers (do not alter meaning), highlight/climax identification, narrative coherence and creator-brand pacing.
- Auto-select promising moments against topic and actual audience evidence; generate at least materially different editorial variants and explain their differences; never use an unverifiable "viral score" as fact.
- Intelligent reframing/face/subject tracking, safe-area overlays, dynamic accurate captions and optional translated subtitles, lawful licensed music/sounds, original or licensed B-roll, tasteful transitions, color consistency/correction, professional audio cleanup/loudness, thumbnails and platform-specific endings/CTAs.
- Formats include applicable 9:16, 4:5, 1:1 and 16:9, and supported 1080p/4K outputs where input/device/render support them; platform-specific encoding, sound sync, text safe zones, render provenance, and unbranded/unwatermarked files unless user explicitly chooses permitted branding.
- Optimize outcomes, not just visual noise: preserve authentic speech/meaning, no deceptive cuts, avoid excessive jump cuts, avoid repetitive intro/hook across recent posts and detect legal restrictions (e.g. sponsor exclusivity, music licensing, unauthorized third-party likeness).
- Natural-language and voice requests via full ZERO: "create three on-brand reels from last Friday's vlog informed by this week's real niche opportunities"; production-grade tool execution with job status/retry/cancel, previews and verified downloadable exports.
- Local-first editing/transcode where measured quality meets best-in-class; the founder RTX 5090 is a compute worker, not the only online render resource. Heavy hosted media tasks must remain accessible independent of home power/network outages with durable queue/fallback, clear limits and cost metering.

## F. Hyper-quality AUTO EMAIL OPERATOR — actually sends safely

- Real OAuth-integrated Gmail and Microsoft 365 mailboxes with verified scope, correct per-account identity, thread consistency, real sender address, attachment safety, provider time/rate limits, durable delivery outcome and API receipts.
- Learn creator-approved voice/register from historic **authorized own** sent drafts/replies + editor corrections, explicit rate cards and communication policy, language, brand preferences, signature, approved offers, contact relationship, calendar slots and prior deal obligations. Never make up the creator's rates, availability, signed contracts, financial status or attachments.
- Read/analyze/filter important threads, remove irrelevant/spam from the action queue (do not delete mail without permission), detect phishing/prompt injection, identify sponsor inquiries, fans, PR, event invites, agencies, invoices, scheduling and urgent issues. Handle thread context and reply recipients/CC accurately.
- **Tiered autopilot**: 
  - `AUTOSEND_ALLOWED`: narrow, per-user configurable low-risk categories (simple acknowledgements, information requests already grounded in an approved knowledge base, unambiguous scheduling proposals inside actual free/busy policies) may be sent automatically with explicit prior consent, safe templates, receipts, confidence thresholds and opt-out.
  - `DRAFT_REVIEW`: meaningful brand conversations, negotiated pricing, uncertain facts, sensitive context, publicity and reputational impact: compose a high-quality personalized draft for creator/manager approval.
  - `EXPLICIT_APPROVAL_REQUIRED`: commitments/contracts, exclusivity, rights, advertising spend, promises, legal/financial dispute, personal/sensitive details, higher-risk disclosures. No automatic commitment even if the email model feels confident.
  - `BLOCK_OR_ESCALATE`: impersonation, phishing, unsupported access, missing source facts, anomalous attachments, suspicious prompt instructions.
- No inbox noise, no reply loops, no repeated replies after a timeout; idempotent exactly-one-intended-send semantics with outcome reconciliation where provider supports it. Pause on ambiguous send receipt rather than repeatedly sending; audit every send with identity, evidence and policy.
- Response quality checks: factual accuracy, address/recipient, sender style, proper locale, directness, empathy/social intelligence, completeness, correct scheduling and obligations, spelling/grammar, polite decline where needed, disclosure and non-spam behavior.
- Zero unapproved high-risk send, zero confirmed unauthorized data disclosure, zero fabricated deal commitments in mandatory adversarial checks; measurable human-reviewed quality/parity against competing professional email workflows on representative samples.
- Production app OAuth verification/security assessment may be required: Google Gmail scopes such as read/modify can be **restricted** and external app verification may be necessary. An early demo with mocked permissions cannot satisfy commercial acceptance.
