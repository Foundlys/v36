## A. Creator V1 — ACTUAL sellable product
A premium personal AI manager that handles authorized scheduling, communication, sponsor relationships, revenue tracking, social publishing where official provider access allows, and source-evidenced content growth strategy on desktop and supported phone UI.

- **ZERO Personal Manager:** personalized voice/conversation, trusted authorized long-term context, task/deadline attention, natural social/empathy modes and secure real tool execution. Do not claim autonomous real-world effects without provider receipts.
- **TODAY / WEEKLY content strategist:** real source-backed rising subjects, predicted *relative* creator/topic fit, historical post analytics, evidenced timing, idea briefs, hooks, scripts, shot lists, calendar tasks and sponsor fit. No invented trends or guaranteed virality.
- **Email autopilot:** bounded low-risk automatic real replies in creator-approved voice, consented actual inbox access and provider send receipts; sponsor price/contract/right commitments require explicit approval.
- **Calendar / tasks:** availability, bookable slots and checked authorized Google/Microsoft event operations; avoid duplicate appointments and false external sync receipts.
- **Brand Deals CRM + Media Kit / Rate Card:** opportunities, sponsors, negotiations, deadlines, deliverables, invoices and contractual rights with human authorization for commitments.
- **Social workspace:** actual permitted creator account/channel analytics, post history, scripts/drafts, external existing asset references, a publishing calendar, supported platform publishing and verifiable publication state; no fictional provider data/API entitlement.
- **Agency permissions:** per-creator scoped managers/editors/accountants, consent, audit and no cross-creator content or account leakage. "Editor" here is a future delegated role, not proof that V1 ships a video/photo editor.
- **Native value:** on-brand actionable guidance and end-to-end business workflows, **not** producing, editing or rendering creative media. Creators can publish previously produced/uploaded media through authorized official integrations.

Example only where independently verified connected data exists: "Yesterday's actual posted videos in your niche had higher watched-through rates in your chosen audience region. Your own top three recent posts did better on topic A. Today draft topic A with hook B and schedule shooting with sponsor deadline C. I can reply to this low-risk booking email under your approved policy." A proposal/slot/queue isn't proof of a sent/published action.

## C. REAL MARKET-DATA OPPORTUNITY ENGINE — daily and weekly

**Inputs (only as licensed/authorized/available):**
1. Creator first-party account analytics, historic publish/performance and channel/audience data from provider-approved APIs; never substitute simulated provider results.
2. Current niche/location/language/topic/search momentum from accessible licensed trends/search providers. Google Trends API is alpha/invite-only until confirmed otherwise, so **cannot be assumed available**.
3. TikTok Creative Center/Trends and other publicly viewable trend signals only via compliant permitted use; website visibility DOES NOT grant authorized high-volume API scraping. TikTok Research API is researcher-restricted, **not** a default commercial ingestion API.
4. YouTube channel/video analytics via creator OAuth where applicable (views, engagement, retention/watch duration, traffic source, follower changes and other actually accessible metrics), plus compliant approved metadata from other channels.
5. Brand deals, posting contractual obligations, upcoming events/holidays relevant to region, creator calendar/availability, creator-supplied references to existing assets, capacity and editorial safety.

**Workflow:**
- Refresh sources as frequently as license, source rate limit, latency budget and actual data cadence allow; record observation and ingestion timestamps, permissions and missing fields; avoid labels like "live" for delayed metrics.
- Compare trend movement and creator historical performance with cohort/format baseline and statistical uncertainty. Normalize platform metric definitions and observation windows, avoid cross-platform unqualified views/engagement comparisons and future-information leakage.
- Score each possible concept with transparent, editable rubric: creator/content-fit, current evidenced demand/momentum, historical viewer response, saturation/fatigue, originality, production effort, risk/legal/brand compatibility, deadlines and confidence/data quality.
- For each proposed opportunity, produce a **source-linked evidence card**: exact observed metric/window, assumptions, relative rationale, uncertainty, confidence classification, expected effort, recommended format/length/hook/shot list/caption/thumbnail brief/language, creator-owned asset references already on hand, recommended publication window and permission requirements.
- Every morning: a ranked actionable **TODAY** brief (3–5 materially differentiated content/video/photo/story *ideas and briefs* (without editing)). Every week: a calendar-aware **WEEKLY** plan with realistic production tasks, 7-day schedule, repurpose queue, sponsor deadlines and capacity balance.
- Do not endlessly chase trend content: balance brand identity, sustainable cadence, audience fatigue, niche consistency, original content and sponsor obligations.
- Outcome loop: capture only authorized actual post performance, compare to comparable historic posts, record prediction error/confounding, recalibrate opportunity model and content planning recommendations. A forecast score is **not** an empirically calibrated "virality probability" until validated prospectively.
- A/B variant experiment plans may be prepared, but publication and outcome tracking respect provider limitations; do not label observational comparison as causal proof.

## F. Hyper-quality AUTO EMAIL OPERATOR — actually sends safely

- Real OAuth-integrated Gmail and Microsoft 365 mailboxes with verified scope, correct per-account identity, thread consistency, real sender address, attachment safety, provider time/rate limits, durable delivery outcome and API receipts.
- Learn creator-approved voice/register from historic **authorized own** sent drafts/replies + editor corrections, explicit rate cards and communication policy, language, brand preferences, signature, approved offers, contact relationship, calendar slots and prior deal obligations. Never make up the creator's rates, availability, signed contracts, financial status or attachments.
- Read/analyze/filter important threads, remove irrelevant/spam from the action queue (do not delete mail without permission), detect phishing/prompt injection, identify sponsor inquiries, fans, PR, event invites, agencies, invoices, scheduling and urgent issues. Handle thread context and reply recipients/CC accurately.
- **Tiered autopilot**: 
  - `AUTOSEND_ALLOWED`: narrow, per-user configurable low-risk categories (simple acknowledgements, information requests already grounded in an approved knowledge base, unambiguous scheduling proposals inside actual free/busy policies) may be sent automatically with explicit prior consent, safe templates, receipts, confidence thresholds and opt-out.
  - `DRAFT_REVIEW`: meaningful brand conversations, negotiated pricing, uncertain facts, sensitive context, publicity and reputational impact: compose a high-quality personalized draft for creator/manager approval.
  - `EXPLICIT_APPROVAL_REQUIRED`: commitments/contracts, exclusivity, rights, advertising spend, promises, legal/financial dispute, personal/sensitive details, higher-risk disclosures. No automatic commitment even if the email model feels confident.
  - `BLOCK_OR_ESCALATE`: impersonation, phishing, unsupported access, missing source facts, anomalous attachments, suspicious prompt instructions.
- No inbox noise, no reply loops, no repeated replies after a timeout; idempotent exactly-one-intended-send semantics with outcome reconciliation where provider supports it. Pause on ambiguous send receipt rather than repeatedly sending; audit every send with identity, evidence and policy.
- Response quality checks: factual accuracy, address/recipient, sender style, proper locale, directness, empathy/social intelligence, completeness, correct scheduling and obligations, spelling/grammar, polite decline where needed, disclosure and non-spam behavior.
- Zero unapproved high-risk send, zero confirmed unauthorized data disclosure, zero fabricated deal commitments in mandatory adversarial checks; measurable human-reviewed quality/parity against competing professional email workflows on representative samples.
- Production app OAuth verification/security assessment may be required: Google Gmail scopes such as read/modify can be **restricted** and external app verification may be necessary. An early demo with mocked permissions cannot satisfy commercial acceptance.
